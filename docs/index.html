The Alignment Veto:
How Safety Training Suppresses
Cultural Knowledge in LLMs

Pardis Sadat Zahraei  ·  Nizi Nazar  ·  Ehsaneddin Asgari

Across 16 MENA countries, 26 models, and 1.53M human survey responses, we show that when a model refuses a culturally sensitive question, the knowledge is suppressed — not erased. The model's internal logits at refusal correlate with human data more than its freely generated answers.

The Alignment Veto

Standard assumption: a refused question means missing knowledge.
Our finding: the knowledge is present — but blocked.

The model's internal logit distributions at the moment of refusal correlate with human survey data more strongly than its freely generated answers on non-refused items — refused T3 EV-NVAS 0.718 vs. accepted T3 NVAS 0.690 (Δ=+0.029, p<10⁻¹³).

The Safety Tax

Instruction-tuned models refuse T3 sensitive questions at 23.9% vs. 12.4% for benign T1 questions — a +11.5% safety tax (Cohen's d=0.303, p<10⁻¹¹⁵). The distribution is highly unequal.

Safety Tax by Model (T3 − T1 refusal rate)

Scale does not predict the tax

Spearman ρ=0.147 (p=0.464) between parameter count and safety tax. OLMo-32B-IT: 25.3% tax. GPT-5: −2.9% tax. Size is irrelevant; alignment pipeline is what matters.

DPO stage installs the tax

Within OLMo-3 and Tulu-3 families, safety tax increases monotonically: Base → SFT → DPO → IT (p<0.05 within-family, paired). DPO adds suppression; IT adds more.

The Gate Is Inequitable

A 19.8% alignment-quality gap separates best- and worst-served nations. Hover cards for details.

Color scale: red = low T3 NVAS (worst-served) → teal = high T3 NVAS (best-served). Palestine has highest T3 NVAS (0.731) and highest T3 refusal (0.352) — consistent with the alignment veto: accurate internal representation, high suppression.

Native Language Hurts

Switching from English to Arabic/Persian/Turkish drops mean NVAS by −0.050 points consistently across all 26 models — including Arabic-specialized models.

Residualized Probing: Late-Layer Inversion

Probing for cross-country cultural variation after subtracting per-question means reveals a two-phase trajectory in Tulu-3-8B, OLMo-7B, and LLaMA-8B:

Early layers (2–3): cultural info enters (R²=+0.06 to +0.14) Late layers (28–31): alignment counteracts it (R² turns negative)
